Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Ticketa free to use? +
Yes. Browsing and starting a ticket is free. Each offer is run by a third-party partner, and their own terms apply once you continue to their site.
Who fulfills these offers? +
Ticketa doesn't fulfill offers directly. Each card above links to an advertising partner (e.g. CTConnect, SuperSweepstakes, PrizeZappy) who runs and fulfills that specific offer.
Why do tickets change? +
Our partners rotate availability based on demand and region, so the list updates regularly. If a ticket is unavailable, check back soon for a new one.
Do I need to make a purchase? +
No purchase is required to view or start a ticket. Some partner offers may involve optional steps like sign-ups — always read the partner's terms before continuing.
